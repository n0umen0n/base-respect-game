# Consortium Lab & DAO OF THE APES

## 0:00 – 0:25 — The Core Obsession

Hi, I'm Vlad, founder of DAO OF THE APES and Consortium Lab.

I'm obsessed with one question:
**How do we design governance systems such that humans, working together, become maximally productive?**

Human progress depends on social organizations. And governance determines whether effort compounds — or dissolves into politics, misalignment, and friction.

## 0:25 – 0:55 — The Concrete Problem: Token / Equity Misalignment

Crypto introduced a powerful primitive: tokens.
But it never developed a coherent organizational structure for token holders.

In traditional systems, equity holders operate through entities: LLCs, corporations, partnerships. Their rights, responsibilities, and governance frameworks are clearly defined.

In crypto, token holders often have economic exposure — but no structured coordination system.

This creates recurring tension between equity and token holders, between foundations and communities, between long-term builders and short-term capital.

We've seen governance friction emerge in protocols like Aave, Uniswap, and Arbitrum.

Capital formation scaled faster than governance design.

## 0:55 – 1:35 — The Gap

There are dozens of legal entities optimized for equity.
There is no widely accepted, productivity-optimized entity for tokenized communities.

Token holders are economically powerful — but the organizations behind their tokens often lack structural integrity.

When the organization governing a token is weak, token holders bear the cost — diminished trust, higher risk, and productivity that decays instead of compounds.

Tokens have proven their economic power. What's missing is the organizational layer that makes that power sustainable — and compounds it over time.

The question becomes:

**What is the right organizational form for the AI and crypto age?**

## 1:35 – 2:15 — The Solution: Consortium Lab

That is why we founded Consortium Lab.

Consortium Lab researches, designs, and develops new organizational structures optimized for the age of crypto and AI.

DAO OF THE APES is one of our live experimental environments.

The goal is to define and formalize a new organizational primitive — a **consortium** — a structured coordination entity for token holders that reduces friction, aligns incentives, and maximizes productivity over time.

## 2:15 – 2:50 — Vision

As AI systems automate execution, governance becomes the critical layer that determines direction, legitimacy, and resilience.

Technology is accelerating.
But without better organizational structures, human and AI coordination will remain the bottleneck.

Consortium Lab exists to design governance systems fit for the AI and crypto age — systems where effort compounds, legitimacy scales, and organizations remain productive as they grow.

**Technology evolves fast. Organizational design must catch up.
Consortium Lab exists to close that gap.**
